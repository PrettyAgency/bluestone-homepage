03 / About

About BlueStone

Built for Bendigo ground.

A lot of Bendigo is not flat, not dry and not easy.

BlueStone Landscape Construction is led by founder Scott Bateson, who has spent more than 19 years in landscaping, in Australia and overseas. As registered building practitioners we can take on the engineered and permit-required work many landscapers turn away, and you deal with a builder who is accountable for the result.

Steep blocks, windy exposure, reactive clay and low corners that hold water all winter: we have spent two decades learning what holds, drains and grows on this ground, and we design around it rather than against it. New house or existing garden, we plan it, build it and finish it as one team.

01What landscaping services do you offer in Bendigo?General

Retaining walls, paving, turf and synthetic grass, decking and pergolas, drainage and excavation, irrigation, stone and rock work, water features, garden lighting and planting, plus full landscape construction that brings it all together for a new build or an existing garden.

02Which areas do you cover?General

We are based in Bendigo and work across the city and surrounding areas, including Strathfieldsaye, Junortoun, Maiden Gully, Kangaroo Flat, Golden Square, Eaglehawk, Epsom, Huntly and Strathdale. Further out? Call and ask; the answer is usually yes.

03Can you work on sloping or difficult blocks?General

That's a large part of what we do. Steep sites, poor access, heavy clay and wet ground all need a different approach to planning, drainage and machinery, and we've built on all of them around Bendigo.

04Who will actually be working on my project?General

BlueStone is led by founder Scott Bateson. One team runs your job from start to finish with our own machinery, so you are not passed between subcontractors and you always know who to call.

05How much experience do you have?General

Scott has spent more than 19 years in landscaping, in Australia and overseas, and BlueStone are registered building practitioners, so we can take on the engineered work many landscapers turn away.

06Do you landscape new homes?General

Yes. New builds are a big part of our work: levelling, retaining, drainage, paving, turf and planting, planned together so the yard is finished properly rather than pieced together over years.

07Can you update part of an existing garden?General

Absolutely. Plenty of our jobs are a new patio, a replaced retaining wall or a fresh lawn in an established yard. We work around what you want to keep.

08Do I need to be home while you work?General

Not usually. We need clear access, pets secured and an idea of where we can use water and power. We will talk through anything specific to your site before we start.

09Will you leave my property tidy?General

Yes. We leave a tidy site at the end of every working day, and at handover we walk the finished job with you and show you how to look after it.

10How do I get started?General

Call 0400 078 913 or send the quote form. A couple of photos and rough measurements help us give you an early steer before we come out.

11Do you provide free quotes?Quotes

Yes. We come to your property, look at the site and give you an itemised written quote with no obligation.

12How much does landscaping cost in Bendigo?Quotes

Every yard is different, so we price from a site visit rather than guess over the phone. The itemised quote shows exactly where the money goes, so you can adjust scope or materials before you commit.

13What affects the price of a landscaping job?Quotes

The big drivers are access for machinery, slope, soil type, the materials you choose, how much excavation and drainage is needed, and whether the work needs engineering or a permit.

14What does an itemised quote include?Quotes

A breakdown by stage and item, such as excavation, drainage, walls, paving and turf, with materials described, so you can see what each part of the job costs and compare like with like.

15How quickly will I get my quote?Quotes

We aim to turn quotes around promptly after the site visit. Larger jobs that need engineering or design input can take a little longer, and we will tell you up front if so.

16Can you work to a set budget?Quotes

Yes. Tell us your budget early and we will suggest where to spend and where to save, from material choices to staging the work.

17Can the work be done in stages?Quotes

Often, yes. A common approach is to do the structural work first (excavation, retaining and drainage) and add paving, turf and planting later, without having to undo anything.

18What should I prepare before the site visit?Quotes

Photos, rough measurements, any house or site plans, pictures of yards you like and a budget range. Even a sketch on paper helps us understand what you have in mind.

19Do you build retaining walls?Walls

Yes: concrete sleeper, stone and rock, timber and block walls, from low garden walls to engineered terracing. See our retaining walls page for materials, drainage and permits.

20Which retaining wall material is best?Walls

It depends on height, load, look and budget. Concrete sleepers are tidy and durable, natural stone and rock suit a softer look, block walls handle curves well and timber is the budget option for low walls. We will recommend what suits your site.

21Do retaining walls need drainage?Walls

Yes, almost always. Water building up behind a wall is the main reason walls lean and fail, so we install drainage pipe and free-draining backfill behind every wall we build.

22Does my retaining wall need an engineer?Walls

Taller walls, and walls holding up a driveway, building or steep slope, generally need engineering. As registered building practitioners we can build engineered walls, and we will tell you at the quote whether yours needs it.

23Can you replace a leaning or failing wall?Walls

Yes. We remove the old wall safely, work out why it failed (usually drainage or footings) and rebuild it so the same problem does not come back.

24What paving options do you offer?Paving

Natural stone such as sandstone and bluestone, ashlar and random-cut patterns, and concrete pavers in a wide range of colours. We will bring samples and talk through slip resistance, heat and upkeep.

25How do you stop pavers sinking or moving?Paving

The work happens underneath: a properly excavated and compacted base, the right bedding layer, falls that shed water, and solid edge restraint so the paving holds its shape.

26Can you pave around a pool?Paving

Yes. Pool surrounds and coping are a specialty, with stone and pavers chosen for slip resistance and comfort underfoot, and falls that keep water away from the pool and house.

27Do you build stone feature walls?Paving

Yes. Stacked natural stone feature walls, cladding and rock work, with lighting worked in if you want the wall to come alive at night.

28Can you install a water feature?Paving

Yes. From simple bubbling features to built-in stone pieces, planned with power and drainage from the start.

29Can paving go straight onto my existing lawn?Paving

No. Grass and topsoil need to come out so the base sits on firm ground. Skipping that step is why cheap paving sinks within a couple of years.

30Do you install natural and synthetic turf?Gardens

Yes. We supply and lay instant turf suited to Bendigo conditions, and install synthetic grass where shade, traffic or water make real lawn impractical.

31Which turf variety suits Bendigo?Gardens

Varieties that handle hot, dry summers and frosty winters do best, and buffalo, couch and kikuyu types are common choices. Sun, shade, kids and dogs all change the answer, so we recommend a variety once we see the yard.

32How do I look after new turf?Gardens

Keep it well watered for the first few weeks until the roots take hold, stay off it as much as you can, and hold off mowing until it is firmly rooted. We will give you care instructions on the day.

33When is the best time to lay turf?Gardens

Spring and autumn are ideal, but turf can be laid most of the year with the right watering. In the middle of summer it simply needs more attention early on.

34Do you install irrigation?Gardens

Yes. Drip irrigation for garden beds and sprinkler systems for lawns, set up so water goes where plants need it and not onto paths and fences.

35Do you install garden lighting?Gardens

Yes. Path, step, feature and wall lighting that makes the yard safer and usable after dark. Planning it before paving goes down saves cutting things up later.

36Do you do planting and garden beds?Gardens

Yes. Garden beds, soil preparation, mulch and planting, with species picked for Bendigo's climate and how much time you want to spend gardening.

37Do you build decks and pergolas?Decks

Yes. Timber and composite decks, pergolas and outdoor structures, built as registered building practitioners so they are designed and constructed properly.

38Timber or composite decking: which is better?Decks

Timber looks and feels natural and costs less up front but needs regular oiling. Composite costs more but needs far less maintenance. We will walk you through both with samples.

39Do you build privacy screens?Decks

Yes. Horizontal timber slat screens and other privacy screens, built to match your deck, fencing or house.

40Do I need a permit for a deck?Decks

As a general guide in Victoria, decks more than 800mm above ground, or close to a boundary, usually need a building permit. We will confirm what applies to your design before work starts.

41Can you fix a drainage problem?Drainage

Usually, yes. Most Bendigo drainage problems come down to clay that won't take water and nowhere for it to go. We install ag drains, subsurface drains and grading that moves water away from the house, the lawn and any retaining walls.

42Why does my yard stay soggy after rain?Drainage

Heavy clay soils common around Bendigo drain slowly, and poor grading sends water to the wrong place. The fix is usually a mix of regrading and subsurface drainage, which we can diagnose on site.

43Do you do excavation and site cuts?Drainage

Yes, with our own machinery. Site cuts, levelling, trenching and bulk excavation for new builds, pools, walls and paving.

44Can you get machinery into a tight backyard?Drainage

Often, yes. Let us know your narrowest access point (side gate or path width) when you get in touch and we will plan the job around it.

45Where does the drained water go?Drainage

It needs to go to a legal point of discharge, usually the property's stormwater system. Some connections must be done by a licensed plumber, and we will tell you up front if your job needs one.

46Are you a registered building practitioner?Permits

Yes. Founder Scott Bateson is a registered building practitioner, registration DB-L 39928, with the Building and Plumbing Commission (formerly the Victorian Building Authority). That means BlueStone can build structural landscapes such as engineered retaining walls and decks, and you can check the registration on the public register yourself.

47Do I need a permit for a retaining wall?Permits

As a general guide, walls over about 1 metre high, or walls supporting a driveway, building or boundary, often need a building permit and engineering. Rules vary by site, so we check before we quote.

48Can you help with permits and engineering?Permits

Yes. We will tell you what your project needs and help organise the engineering and paperwork, so you are not left to sort it out alone.

49Do you check for underground services before digging?Permits

Yes. We check for underground services before any excavation, because hitting a gas, water or power line is dangerous and expensive.

50Does pool landscaping affect my pool fence compliance?Permits

It can. In Victoria, pool barriers must comply with safety standards, and new paving, walls, planters or steps near the fence can create climbable points. We plan the landscaping with the barrier rules in mind.
